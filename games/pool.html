  1. BREAK IT OPENThe table stays open after the break. Your first legal solid or stripe after that chooses your group.
  2. CLEAR YOUR CREWHit your group first. Pocket one to keep shooting. If nothing drops, a ball must reach a rail after contact.
  3. CALL YOUR EIGHTClear your seven, pick a numbered pocket, then sink the 8. An early 8, a wrong pocket, or scratching with it loses the rack.

YOUR SHOT. YOUR WAY.

Tap or drag anywhere on the cloth to aim. Set power with the slider, then press SHOOT. The curved-arrow buttons make tiny aiming adjustments.

After a foul: move the white ball to a clear spot and press PLACE CUE. On a keyboard, use the arrows to move it and Enter to confirm.

Keyboard: ←/→ aim · Shift for finer aim · ↑/↓ power · Space shoot · P pause.

Scoring & arcade rules

Legal pots score 100 plus a run bonus of up to 100 per ball. Win the rack for 1,000 more. CPU victories qualify for device-local records; practice and two-player tables keep separate match scores.

Ordinary pots are automatically called. If both groups drop on an open table, the first legal pot decides your group. The 8 is always respotted on the break. A scratch or illegal break gives the incoming player cue-ball placement anywhere. No jump shots or spin.
